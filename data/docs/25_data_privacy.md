# Data Privacy for Customer Records

Customer records contain personal data and must be handled under privacy law.
Collect only what is needed to deliver service: name, address, contact details,
and service history. Do not store payment card numbers; use the tokenized payment
processor instead.

Access to customer records is role based. Dispatchers and technicians see only
the records needed for their work. Exporting customer lists requires manager
approval and a documented business purpose. Records are retained for seven years
and then securely destroyed.

Data breaches must be reported immediately to the privacy officer and the
director of operations. Customers are notified as required by law. Staff complete
annual privacy training. Records are never shared with third parties except
subcontractors performing the service under a confidentiality agreement, and
marketing use requires explicit opt-in consent.
