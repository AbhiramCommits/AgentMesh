# Insurance and Liability Coverage

The company carries general liability, commercial auto, and workers compensation
insurance. General liability covers third-party property damage and bodily injury
up to the policy limit. Commercial auto covers service vehicles and on-road
accidents. Workers compensation covers employee injuries on the job.

Technicians must report any accident, property damage, or injury immediately.
Damage to customer property is documented with photos and a written incident
report. Claims are submitted to the insurer within 48 hours. Do not admit
liability or promise payment on the scene.

Subcontractors must carry their own liability and workers compensation coverage
with certificates on file. Additional insured endorsements are required for
high-value commercial contracts. Certificates of insurance are renewed annually
and tracked by the risk coordinator. Coverage limits and exclusions are reviewed
with counsel each year.
