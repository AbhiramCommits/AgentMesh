# Discount and Promotion Policy

Discounts are applied only through approved promotion codes. Standard promotions
include a new customer diagnostic waiver and a seasonal tune-up special. The
maximum stackable discount is 20 percent, and a manager override is required
above that. Discounts cannot be combined with warranty repairs, which are already
free of labor.

Promotional codes carry an expiry date and a redemption limit. The marketing team
publishes the active promotion calendar monthly. Support agents verify code
eligibility before applying it and record the code on the ticket for audit.

Loyalty and referral discounts are defined in the loyalty terms. Employee
friends-and-family discounts require supervisor approval. All discounting is
reported weekly, and margin impact is reviewed by finance. Unauthorized discounts
are reversed and addressed through coaching.
