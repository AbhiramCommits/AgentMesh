# Dispatcher Shift Handover

Dispatcher shift handover happens at 7am and 3pm. The outgoing dispatcher briefs
the incoming dispatcher on open emergencies, technicians running late, parts
shortages, and customers waiting on callbacks. The briefing uses a standard
handover template in the dispatch console.

The handover template lists active incidents with status, unassigned tickets
approaching SLA breach, and any VIP accounts needing white-glove treatment. The
incoming dispatcher acknowledges each item and owns it for the shift.

Shift notes are timestamped and visible to the field supervisors. Critical items,
such as a technician with no parts or a second missed appointment, must be
escalated in the handover rather than left for the next dispatcher to discover.
Unresolved handover items are reviewed at the daily standup. A clean handover is
measured by zero dropped callbacks per shift.
