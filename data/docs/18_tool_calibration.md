# Tool and Equipment Calibration

Measuring tools are calibrated annually to maintain accuracy. This includes
multimeters, refrigerant gauges, combustion analyzers, manometers, and torque
wrenches. Calibration certificates are filed in the equipment log and a sticker
records the next due date on each tool.

Tools out of calibration are removed from service and sent to the calibration
vendor. Technicians must not use a tool whose calibration has expired. New tools
are entered into the asset register and assigned to a technician or vehicle.

Combustion analyzers require calibration every six months and a bump test before
each heating season. Refrigerant scales are verified quarterly. Lost or damaged
tools are reported and replaced through the tool crib. Equipment downtime and
calibration spend are tracked on the operations scorecard, with a target of 98
percent of tools within calibration at all times.
