# Emergency Dispatch Rules

Emergency dispatch covers situations that threaten property, health, or safety,
including gas leaks, sewage backup, electrical arcing, and burst pipes. When an
emergency call arrives, the dispatcher must confirm the address, capture a
callback number, and assign the nearest available qualified technician, even if
that technician is currently on a non-emergency job.

Emergency jobs preempt routine appointments. The dispatcher notifies affected
customers whose appointment windows shift and offers the next available slot.
If no technician is within the 2 hour emergency radius, the dispatcher escalates
to the on-call supervisor, who may authorize a subcontractor.

Every emergency dispatch generates an incident log capturing time received, time
dispatched, technician assigned, and outcome. The safety checklist must be read
aloud to the customer before the technician enters the premises. After the visit,
the field lead reviews the incident log within 24 hours.
