# Appliance Repair Playbook

Appliance repair covers refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens, and
ranges. Start each visit by confirming the model and serial number, then check
power, water supply, and error codes before disassembly. Many failures trace to a
clogged filter, a stuck door switch, or a tripped breaker.

For refrigerators, test the compressor, defrost heater, and thermistor. For
washers, inspect the drain pump, lid switch, and drive belt. Dryers need lint
path clearing and thermal fuse checks. Dishwashers usually fail on the drain
pump or the float switch. Ovens and ranges require igniter, element, and gas
valve checks.

Order parts by model number to avoid mismatches. If the repair exceeds 50 percent
of replacement cost, present a replacement option. Document error codes and
readings in the work order. Always test a full cycle before leaving the site.
