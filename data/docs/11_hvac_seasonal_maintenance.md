# HVAC Seasonal Maintenance Plan

Our HVAC seasonal maintenance plan keeps systems efficient across heating and
cooling seasons. Residential plans include two visits per year: a spring cooling
tune-up and a fall heating tune-up. Commercial plans include quarterly visits and
belt, filter, and coil inspections.

The cooling tune-up checks refrigerant charge, cleans condenser coils, tests
capacitors and contactors, and verifies thermostat calibration. The heating
tune-up inspects the heat exchanger, tests ignition and flame sensors, checks gas
pressure, and replaces filters. Combustion analysis is required on gas furnaces.

Maintenance visits are scheduled automatically from the contract anniversary.
Plans include a 15 percent parts discount and front-of-line scheduling during
peak season. Deferred maintenance findings are documented with photos and a
recommended repair quote. Members receive a seasonal readiness report by email.
