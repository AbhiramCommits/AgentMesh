# Technician Onboarding Checklist

New technicians complete a two week onboarding before working solo. Week one
covers safety orientation, tool kit assignment, and shadowing a senior
technician on ride-alongs. Week two covers our diagnostic software, the ticket
and dispatch app, and customer communication standards.

Required certifications include a valid driver's license, EPA Section 608 for
refrigerant handling, and state licensing where applicable. Technicians must pass
a background check and a drug screen. Personal protective equipment is issued on
day one: gloves, safety glasses, and steel-toe boots.

Onboarding includes a written skills assessment and a supervised solo visit
observed by a field lead. The field lead signs off before the technician is added
to the on-call rotation. Each new hire is paired with a mentor for the first 60
days and receives a 30 day and 90 day performance review.
