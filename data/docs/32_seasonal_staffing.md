# Seasonal Staffing Plan

Demand peaks in summer for cooling and winter for heating. The seasonal staffing
plan adds temporary technicians and dispatchers ahead of each peak. Hiring begins
90 days before the peak with a target of 20 percent additional field capacity.

Temporary technicians complete an abbreviated onboarding focused on safety and
the most common call types. They are paired with senior technicians and are not
added to the on-call rotation until signed off. Dispatcher reinforcements handle
overflow calls and after-hours coverage.

Overtime is capped at 10 hours per technician per week to reduce fatigue and
safety risk. Contractor partners provide surge capacity when internal capacity is
exhausted. Staffing forecasts use three years of call volume history plus weather
projections. Post-season, temporary staff are evaluated for conversion to
full-time roles. Workforce metrics are reviewed monthly by operations.
