# Parts Inventory Management

Each service vehicle stocks a standard parts kit and is audited weekly. The
warehouse tracks inventory by part number, bin location, and reorder point.
When stock falls below the reorder point, the system raises a purchase order to
the preferred supplier automatically.

High-turnover parts such as capacitors, contactors, thermostats, and common
fittings must always be available. Critical spare parts for warranty repairs are
reserved and tagged so they are not consumed on non-warranty jobs. Technicians
scan parts out of the vehicle kit when installed and scan returns when unused.

Monthly cycle counts reconcile vehicle kits against the inventory system.
Discrepancies above five percent trigger a full audit. Obsolescent parts are
returned to the supplier or written off with supervisor approval. Inventory
accuracy is a key metric on the operations scorecard.
