# Customer Satisfaction Survey Process

A satisfaction survey is sent by text and email within two hours of work order
closure. The survey uses a five star rating plus optional comments. Customers who
rate four or five stars are asked to leave a public review. Customers who rate
three stars or fewer trigger a follow-up call within one business day.

Survey results are aggregated by technician, region, and service type. The
company target is a rolling 30 day average of at least 4.5 stars. Technicians
receive a bonus tied to their individual satisfaction score. Recurring low scores
trigger coaching and, if unresolved, a performance plan.

Negative comments are read by the field supervisor and the customer care team.
Detractors are contacted personally by a manager to resolve the issue. Survey
verbatims are reviewed weekly in the operations meeting and themes are fed back to
training. The response rate is tracked to ensure the sample stays representative.
