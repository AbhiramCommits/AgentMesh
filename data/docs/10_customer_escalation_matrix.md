# Customer Escalation Matrix

Escalation routes a complaint to the right owner based on severity and value.
Level one issues are handled by the assigned support agent. Level two issues,
including missed SLA and repeat visits, escalate to the field supervisor. Level
three issues, such as property damage or safety concerns, escalate to the
regional operations manager.

Financial disputes above 1000 dollars escalate to the finance manager. Legal
threats, media inquiries, and regulatory complaints escalate immediately to the
director of operations and the legal team. Every escalation records the customer,
ticket, reason, owner, and target response time.

The escalation matrix is displayed in the dispatch console. Escalations must be
acknowledged within one hour and updated daily until closed. Unacknowledged
escalations automatically page the on-call manager. Closure requires a written
resolution and a customer confirmation where possible.
