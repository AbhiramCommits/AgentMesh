# Pricing Tiers and Rate Card

AgentMesh field services publishes a transparent rate card. The standard tier
charges a flat 89 dollar diagnostic fee plus 120 dollars per labor hour. The
preferred tier, for customers with an active maintenance contract, charges a 69
dollar diagnostic fee and 105 dollars per labor hour. The platinum tier charges
no diagnostic fee and 95 dollars per labor hour with parts billed at cost plus
ten percent.

After-hours work between 8pm and 6am, and all weekend work, carries a 1.5x
labor multiplier. Holiday work carries a 2x multiplier. Trip charges apply
outside the primary service territory at 1.50 dollars per mile beyond the
boundary.

Estimates over 500 dollars require supervisor approval before work begins.
Materials markup on the standard tier is 25 percent and on the preferred tier is
15 percent. All rates exclude sales tax and permit fees, itemized separately on
the final invoice.
