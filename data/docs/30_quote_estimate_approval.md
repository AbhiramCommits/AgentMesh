# Quote and Estimate Approval Rules

Estimates over 500 dollars require supervisor approval before work begins.
Estimates over 5000 dollars additionally require regional manager approval. The
technician presents a written, itemized estimate covering labor, parts, and any
permit fees, and obtains customer sign-off through the field app.

Change orders that increase the estimate by more than 15 percent require a new
approval. Emergency work may proceed before approval only to stop active damage,
and a follow-up estimate must be approved within 24 hours.

Quotes are valid for 30 days unless a part price changes. Declined quotes are
logged with the reason. Deposit policy: jobs over 2000 dollars require a 30
percent deposit. Estimate accuracy, measured as final invoice versus approved
estimate, is tracked monthly with a target of within 10 percent.
