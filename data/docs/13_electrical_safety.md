# Electrical Safety Procedures

Electrical work begins with lockout and tagout. De-energize the circuit at the
breaker, verify with a non-contact tester, and confirm with a multimeter before
touching conductors. Never assume a wire is dead. Wear insulated gloves and
safety glasses, and use insulated tools rated for the voltage.

Diagnose by checking the panel for tripped breakers, GFCI outlets, and loose
neutrals. Trace circuits to map loads before adding new devices. Arc faults,
scorched insulation, and aluminum wiring are red flags requiring a full
inspection and possible remediation quote.

Permitted work includes outlet replacement, fixture installation, and breaker
replacement. Panel upgrades, service entrance work, and anything beyond the meter
require a licensed electrician and a permit. Document all measurements and
photograph the panel before and after work. Reset and label circuits clearly.
