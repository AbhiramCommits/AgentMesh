# SLA Response Times Policy

Our service level agreement defines response and resolution targets by priority
tier. Critical tickets (no heat, no water, active flooding, or total power loss)
require an on-site technician within 2 hours and resolution within 8 hours. High
priority tickets require a 4 hour response and 24 hour resolution. Medium
priority tickets are handled within one business day, and low priority tickets
within three business days.

Response time is measured from the moment the ticket is created in the dispatch
system to the first technician check-in. Resolution time is measured until the
work order is signed off by the customer. Missed SLA targets are flagged on the
dashboard and reviewed at the weekly operations meeting. Repeat breaches trigger
a root-cause review with the regional manager.

For scheduled maintenance visits the SLA clock starts at the appointment window,
not at ticket creation. Commercial accounts on the platinum plan receive a 30
percent faster response commitment than the standard SLA table.
