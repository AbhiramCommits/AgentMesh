# Loyalty Program Terms

The loyalty program rewards repeat customers with points on every completed work
order. Points accrue at one point per dollar spent on labor and are redeemable
for service credits at 100 points per 10 dollar credit. Points expire 24 months
after they are earned.

Members receive priority scheduling, a birthday tune-up discount, and early
access to seasonal promotions. Commercial accounts accrue points on maintenance
contracts and can share them across multiple locations. Points are not
transferable outside the account and have no cash value.

Referrals earn 500 bonus points when the referred customer completes their first
job. Abuse, including fake accounts or fabricated jobs, results in forfeiture.
The loyalty program may change with 30 days notice. Balances are shown in the
customer portal and on each invoice.
