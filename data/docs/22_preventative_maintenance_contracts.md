# Preventative Maintenance Contracts

Preventative maintenance contracts provide scheduled tune-ups, priority
scheduling, and discounted parts. Contracts are sold in annual and multi-year
terms. The preferred plan includes two visits per year; the commercial plan
includes quarterly visits and a dedicated account manager.

Contract customers receive front-of-line scheduling during peak season and a 15
percent parts discount. Contract revenue is recognized evenly across the term.
Renewal reminders go out 60 days before expiry, and lapsed contracts are offered a
win-back discount.

Each contract specifies covered equipment with make, model, and serial number.
Work outside the covered equipment list is quoted separately. Contracts are
reviewed at renewal for equipment changes. A retention target of 80 percent is
tracked monthly, and accounts at risk of non-renewal are escalated to the account
manager for a save call.
