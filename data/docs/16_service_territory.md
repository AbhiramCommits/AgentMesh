# Service Territory Boundaries

The primary service territory includes the metro core within a 25 mile radius of
the downtown depot. Secondary territories extend to 50 miles and carry a trip
charge. Work beyond 50 miles is quoted individually and may be declined if no
technician is available.

Territory is divided into zones for routing and on-call coverage. Zone A covers
the downtown and north side, Zone B the east and southeast, Zone C the west and
southwest, and Zone D the surrounding suburbs. Each zone has a designated field
lead responsible for quality and scheduling.

Cross-zone dispatch is allowed when the nearest technician is in an adjacent
zone. Travel time across zone boundaries counts toward the appointment window.
New franchise territories require a market study and operations director
approval. Territory maps are updated quarterly and published to the dispatch
console.
