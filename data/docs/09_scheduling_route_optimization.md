# Scheduling and Route Optimization

The scheduling engine assigns appointments by technician skill, territory, and
drive time. Routes are optimized each morning to minimize windshield time while
respecting appointment windows. The goal is no more than 45 minutes of driving
between consecutive jobs.

Appointment windows are two hours wide. Customers receive a reminder text the
evening before and a 30 minute arrival alert when the technician is en route.
Double-booking is prevented by the calendar; the dispatcher may override only
with supervisor approval for emergencies.

Route optimization weighs traffic, distance, and technician certifications. A
technician may only be routed to jobs matching their skill level. If a route
falls behind by more than 30 minutes, the dispatcher rebroadcasts the remaining
jobs to nearby technicians and updates customers proactively. Route efficiency is
reviewed weekly against a target of 85 percent on-time arrivals.
