# Damage Claims Procedure

When our work damages customer property, the technician stops work, secures the
area, and photographs the damage. The technician does not admit fault or promise
compensation. The incident is reported to the dispatcher immediately and logged
as a damage claim ticket linked to the work order.

The claims coordinator contacts the customer within one business day, gathers
documentation, and obtains repair quotes. Small claims under the deductible may
be settled with a goodwill credit or an in-house repair. Larger claims are
submitted to the liability insurer.

The claims coordinator tracks each claim to closure and records the root cause,
such as a dropped tool, a wall scuff, or a plumbing mishap. Root causes are
reviewed monthly to improve training and equipment. Customers receive written
confirmation when a claim is resolved and closed.
