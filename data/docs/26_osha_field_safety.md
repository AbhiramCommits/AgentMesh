# OSHA Field Safety Guidelines

Field safety follows OSHA standards. Technicians wear personal protective
equipment at all times: safety glasses, gloves appropriate to the task, and
steel-toe footwear. Hard hats are required where overhead hazards exist. Fall
protection is mandatory above six feet on ladders and roofs.

Ladder use requires three points of contact and a spotter for extension ladders.
Before working on an occupied roof, the technician confirms roof condition and
uses roof anchors. Electrical work follows lockout and tagout. Gas work requires a
combustible gas detector and ventilation.

Incidents and near misses are reported the same day through the safety app.
Injuries requiring medical attention trigger a formal investigation. Weekly
toolbox talks cover a rotating safety topic. Every vehicle carries a first aid kit
and a fire extinguisher. Safety metrics, including recordable incident rate, are
reviewed monthly by the safety committee.
