# Time Tracking and Payroll Rules

Technicians clock in at the first job and clock out at the last job of the day.
Travel time between jobs is paid; travel from home to the first job is not.
Overtime is paid after 40 hours in a workweek at 1.5x the base rate. On-call
standby time is paid at a flat daily stipend.

Time is captured in the field app and matched to work orders by job number.
Incomplete time entries are flagged and must be corrected before payroll closes
each Monday. Disputed hours are reviewed by the field supervisor with the
technician.

Payroll runs biweekly. Commissions on maintenance contract sales are paid in the
following cycle. Expense reimbursements require receipts and supervisor approval
and are paid with the next payroll. Unapproved overtime is addressed through
coaching, not automatic deduction.
