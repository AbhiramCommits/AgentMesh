# Vendor and Subcontractor Management

Subcontractors are vetted before onboarding: license verification, insurance
certificates, safety record, and reference checks. Approved vendors sign a master
services agreement covering rates, response commitments, and quality standards.

Vendor work is dispatched through the same ticketing system so SLAs and
provenance are preserved. Subcontracted emergency work must be approved by the
on-call supervisor. Vendor invoices are reconciled against work orders before
payment, and discrepancies are disputed in writing.

Vendors are scored quarterly on on-time arrival, first-time fix rate, and
customer satisfaction. Vendors below the quality threshold enter a performance
improvement plan. Repeat failures lead to removal from the approved list. All
vendors must carry general liability and workers compensation insurance with
current certificates on file.
