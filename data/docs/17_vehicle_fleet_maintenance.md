# Vehicle Fleet Maintenance

Service vehicles receive preventative maintenance every 5000 miles or 90 days,
whichever comes first. Maintenance includes oil and filter changes, brake
inspection, tire rotation, and a safety light check. Each vehicle has a log book
in the glovebox and a digital record in the fleet system.

Technicians perform a daily walkaround check: tires, lights, fluid levels, and
the condition of the ladder rack. Any defect must be reported before the vehicle
is driven. Vehicles with safety defects are grounded until repaired.

Fuel cards are assigned per vehicle and reconciled monthly. Mileage is logged
automatically from the GPS telematics unit and matched to work orders. Accident
or damage incidents require an immediate report to the fleet coordinator and a
police report where applicable. Vehicles are replaced on a seven year cycle or at
200,000 miles.
