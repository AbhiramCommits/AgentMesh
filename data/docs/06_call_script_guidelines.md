# Call Script Guidelines

Every inbound call follows a structured greeting, discovery, and close. Open
with the company name, your name, and an offer to help. Confirm the customer's
name, service address, and best callback number before discussing the problem.
Use open questions to let the customer describe symptoms in their own words.

Never diagnose a fault you cannot see. Capture the reported problem, when it
started, and any recent work or weather events. Confirm the property type and
whether the customer owns or rents. Explain the diagnostic fee and any after-hours
multiplier before booking.

Close the call by restating the appointment window, the technician name if known,
and what the customer should prepare, such as clearing access to the unit. Send a
confirmation text and email. Update the ticket with the call summary and any
access instructions so the dispatcher and technician see them.
