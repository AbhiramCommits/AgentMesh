# After-Hours Support Protocol

After-hours support runs from 6pm to 8am on weekdays and 24 hours on weekends
and holidays. A rotating on-call technician answers emergency paging. The
dispatcher triages the call and pages the on-call technician only for emergencies
or high priority tickets.

On-call technicians carry the emergency kit and a fully stocked vehicle. Response
target is one hour for page acknowledgement and two hours on site for critical
issues. Non-emergency calls received after hours are queued for the next business
morning and the customer is offered a first-slot appointment.

After-hours labor is billed at the 1.5x multiplier. The on-call rotation is
published monthly and swaps require supervisor approval. Each on-call shift
produces a handover note listing open incidents, parts used, and follow-ups. The
on-call log is reviewed at the daily operations standup.
