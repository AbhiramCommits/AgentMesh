# Warranty Terms and Coverage

Our labor warranty covers workmanship for 90 days from the invoice date. Parts
installed by our technicians carry the manufacturer warranty, typically 1 year,
and we pass that coverage through to the customer. The warranty does not cover
damage caused by misuse, neglect, unauthorized repairs by third parties, or acts
of nature.

If a covered failure occurs, the customer contacts support with the invoice
number and work order ID. We dispatch a technician at no labor charge and replace
defective parts under the manufacturer warranty. Warranty repairs are prioritized
as high tickets.

Extended warranty plans are available at purchase for an additional fee and
extend labor coverage to 24 months. Warranty claims are logged and reviewed
monthly to detect recurring defects by part number and installation site. Fraud
or repeat claims on the same component may trigger an engineering review.
