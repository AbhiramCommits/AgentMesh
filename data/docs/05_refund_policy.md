# Refund Policy

Refunds are issued when a service fails to meet the agreed scope or when a
diagnostic does not identify a fault. Customers may request a refund within 30
days of the invoice date. The support agent verifies the work order, reviews
technician notes, and confirms whether the complaint is valid.

A full refund of the diagnostic fee is issued if we cannot identify or resolve
the reported problem. A partial refund may be issued when the repair was correct
but a follow-up visit was required due to a scheduling error. Refunds for parts
are governed by the manufacturer return window and may include a restocking fee.

Refund requests are recorded against the original ticket. The finance team
processes approved refunds within five business days to the original payment
method. Denied refunds must include a written explanation and an offer of a
goodwill credit. Escalated refund disputes go to the customer care manager.
