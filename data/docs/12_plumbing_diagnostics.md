# Plumbing Diagnostics Guide

Plumbing diagnostics start with a structured interview and a visual inspection.
Confirm whether the problem is a leak, a clog, low pressure, or no hot water.
Check the meter to detect hidden leaks: if the meter spins with all fixtures
closed, there is a leak in the supply line.

For clogs, locate the blockage with a camera before jetting or snaking. Never
use chemical drain cleaner before a camera inspection. For low pressure, test
fixture flow and inspect the pressure regulator and aerators. For water heaters,
verify the pilot or ignition, the thermocouple, and the anode rod.

Document the diagnosis with photos and a parts list. If the repair requires
permits, such as replacing a water heater or rerouting gas lines, stop and
schedule a licensed specialist. Provide the customer a written estimate before
starting any work over the diagnostic fee.
