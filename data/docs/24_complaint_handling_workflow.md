# Complaint Handling Workflow

Complaints are logged as a distinct ticket type linked to the original work
order. The support agent acknowledges the complaint within one hour, captures the
customer's desired outcome, and assigns an owner. Complaints about safety or
damage are flagged urgent.

The workflow has four stages: acknowledge, investigate, resolve, and confirm.
During investigation the owner reviews technician notes, photos, and parts used,
and may interview the technician. The resolution offers a repair, a refund, or a
goodwill credit according to policy.

Every complaint is closed with a written summary and a customer confirmation.
Complaints that reopen twice escalate to the customer care manager. Root causes
are categorized, such as scheduling, workmanship, communication, or pricing, and
reviewed monthly. A first-contact resolution rate above 70 percent is the
target, and unresolved complaints appear on the escalation dashboard.
